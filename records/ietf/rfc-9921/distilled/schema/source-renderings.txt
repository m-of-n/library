===================================================
This file is VERBATIM: every block below is an exact run of lines from
.cache/rfc-9921.txt. Nothing here is this library's. The filename carries no
`.derived.` infix, which under docs/extraction.md asserts exactly that.

Convention: each region's digest is the sha256 of its lines joined by "\n"
            with no trailing newline.

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. RFC 9921 ships NO formal grammar: `CDDL`, `ABNF`,
`ASN.1`, `JSON Schema`, `schema` and `grammar` each occur ZERO times in the
source. What it does ship is twelve places where it renders a structure by
printing it. Counted by this file's own per-region captions (pass 2 corrected
"four ASN.1 value dumps ... and four byte listings", which did not add up to
what is here): one IANA table, SIX ASN.1 value dumps (the two MessageImprint
results at 311-321 and 369-379, the two TimeStampReqs at 544-556 and 787-799,
and the two truncated TSTs at 562-592 and 805-835), THREE CBOR diagnostic
objects (283-298, 328-349, 769-782) and TWO byte listings (300-309, 351-367).
1 + 6 + 3 + 2 = 12. Those renderings are the document's whole
schema content, so they are captured here exactly, and everything formal in
this directory is this library's and says so in its name.

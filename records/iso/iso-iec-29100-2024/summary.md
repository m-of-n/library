## Overview

The second edition of the ISO/IEC privacy framework. ISO Open Data says it replaces ISO/IEC 29100:2011 and its 2018 amendment. ISO/SAE 21434 cites ISO/IEC 29100 without a date (its Bibliography [25]) as the source for the terms PII and PII principal in its privacy impact criteria (Annex F); the edition current in 2021 was the 2011 one. Added for tmodel RPT-0007 (#11), whose §3.1 uses those criteria.

## Overview

A Publicly Available Specification from the committee behind ISO/SAE 21434, on cybersecurity assurance levels (CAL) and targeted attack feasibility (TAF) for road vehicles. ISO Open Data lists it at stage 60.00 (under publication), with no publication date yet. ISO/SAE 21434 itself introduces CALs only as an informative example scheme in its Annex E (RPT-0007 §3.3). Added for tmodel RPT-0007 (#11), which lists it among the companions of ISO/SAE 21434 (RPT-0007 §1.2).

## Applicability

Bears on DEC-003 (risk-metric scheme), because it covers attack feasibility and assurance levels. Not rated further until read.

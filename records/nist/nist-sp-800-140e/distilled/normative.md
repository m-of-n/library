# NIST SP 800-140E — distilled

- **Role:** Approved authentication mechanisms and their strength requirements for module roles.
- **Modifies / replaces:** ISO/IEC 19790 Annex E and ISO/IEC 24759 §6.17 (replaced).
- **Structure:** Requirements + list for operator authentication strength.
- **Place in the stack:** FIPS 140-3 adopts ISO/IEC 19790 + 24759 and applies the CMVP modifications in the SP 800-140x series; **SP 800-140E** is one of those. See `fips-140-3` and `iso-iec-19790`.

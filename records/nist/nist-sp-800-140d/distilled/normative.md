# NIST SP 800-140D — distilled

- **Role:** The maintained LIST of approved SSP (key) generation and establishment methods.
- **Modifies / replaces:** ISO/IEC 19790 Annex D (replaced).
- **Structure:** A reference list of approved key-management methods; kept current.
- **Place in the stack:** FIPS 140-3 adopts ISO/IEC 19790 + 24759 and applies the CMVP modifications in the SP 800-140x series; **SP 800-140D** is one of those. See `fips-140-3` and `iso-iec-19790`.

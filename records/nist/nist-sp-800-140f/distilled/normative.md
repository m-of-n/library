# NIST SP 800-140F — distilled

- **Role:** Approved test metrics for non-invasive attack (e.g. side-channel) mitigation.
- **Modifies / replaces:** ISO/IEC 19790 Annex F and ISO/IEC 24759 §6.18 (replaced).
- **Structure:** Test-metric requirements for non-invasive attack mitigation.
- **Place in the stack:** FIPS 140-3 adopts ISO/IEC 19790 + 24759 and applies the CMVP modifications in the SP 800-140x series; **SP 800-140F** is one of those. See `fips-140-3` and `iso-iec-19790`.

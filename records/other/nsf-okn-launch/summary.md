## Overview

Announcement of the **live NSF Open Knowledge Network (okn.us)**: a national open-data infrastructure linking **43 interconnected knowledge graphs** and **tens of billions of connected facts** across health, environment, justice, manufacturing, and national security. Its stated purpose is to give AI a **verifiable, attributed, governed knowledge layer** — "a shared, explicit, interoperable, and auditable representation of the world on which [AI] systems are expected to reason and act" — i.e. **grounding, attribution, and knowledge governance** for critical AI.

Grew from the **Proto-OKN program** ($26.7M, 18 teams, 2023) with core support from NIH, NASA, NIJ, NOAA, USGS; each team built a domain KG then connected it through a shared **fabric** into one **federated** network. Now **43 graphs**, **12+ federal agencies**, **90+ cross-sector partnerships**. Flagship cross-graph query: linking supply-chain + manufacturing data to **map cybersecurity exposure across 7,000+ semiconductor products from 247 companies** — supply-chain security as an OKN use case.

## Relevance to tmodel

The single most on-point external reference for tmodel's knowledge-graph thesis: it operationalizes exactly what we want — an **auditable, attributed KG that grounds AI** (vs. plausible-but-unsourced answers), at national scale, **with a supply-chain cybersecurity use case**. Directly informs our object model, provenance/human-review design, and the question of whether/how to **federate** tmodel's threat graph with OKN.
